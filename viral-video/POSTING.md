# Posting kit

**Caption** (keep the question, it drives comments):

> What if SCP-173 was livestreamed? 👁️ Which theory is right: 1, 2 or 3? #scp #scp173 #scpfoundation #whatif #creepy #horrortok #3danimation

**Cover:** the ~0.6 s frame (title fully on screen) or the 31 s frame (the wall of eyes).

**Pinned comment idea:** "Theory 2 is the scariest one and I'll die on this hill" — gets people arguing about which theory wins.

**Handle:** the video is branded `@versionzero.tv` (top-left). To change it, re-render with
`HANDLE=@other ./build.sh`.
